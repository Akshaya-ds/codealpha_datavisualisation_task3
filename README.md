# Sales Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data to uncover meaningful business insights using Python. The dataset was cleaned, explored, and visualized to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue patterns.

The goal of this analysis is to transform raw sales data into actionable insights that can support business decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Understand overall sales performance.
* Identify top-performing product lines.
* Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Discover high-value customers.
* Examine country-wise sales distribution.
* Generate business insights through data visualization.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains sales transaction records with information such as:

* Order Number
* Sales Amount
* Product Line
* Customer Name
* Country
* Order Date
* Deal Size
* Order Status

---

## Tools and Libraries Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## Data Cleaning Process

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

* Checked for missing values
* Identified and handled duplicate records
* Verified data types
* Explored dataset structure and summary statistics

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The analysis includes:

### Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzed sales performance across different months to identify seasonal patterns.

### Product Performance Analysis

Compared sales across product categories to determine the highest-performing product lines.

### Country-wise Sales Analysis

Examined revenue generated by different countries.

### Customer Analysis

Identified top customers based on total sales contribution.

### Deal Size Analysis

Explored the distribution of sales across different deal sizes.

---

## Key Insights

* Certain product lines generated significantly higher revenue than others.
* Sales showed noticeable variations across months, indicating seasonal demand patterns.
* A small group of customers contributed a large portion of total sales.
* Some countries consistently outperformed others in terms of revenue generation.
* Medium and Large deals contributed substantially to overall sales.

---

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of data analysis and visualization techniques to extract valuable insights from sales data. The findings can help businesses better understand customer behavior, optimize product strategies, and improve overall decision-making.
